# Privacy Policy — Tap Orbit

**Last updated:** 27 April 2026

## Summary

**Tap Orbit does not collect, store, or share any personal data.** The app
runs entirely offline on your device.

## What information we collect

**None.** Tap Orbit does not collect, transmit, or share:

- Personal information (name, email, phone, address)
- Device identifiers (advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address)
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other sensor data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports

## What is stored on your device only

The app saves the following locally on your device using Android's
SharedPreferences. This data **never leaves your device** and is not
sent to any server:

- Your highest score
- Whether the audio is enabled or disabled

This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

Tap Orbit requests no special permissions. It does not access the
internet, location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, or any other
sensitive system feature.

## Third-party services

Tap Orbit does **not** include any third-party SDKs, advertising
networks, analytics tools, crash reporting services, or social media
integrations.

## Children's privacy

Tap Orbit is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data,
it complies with COPPA and similar children's-privacy regulations by
default.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, the new version
will be published at the same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
